## Throughput per architecture (median sps over the last 75 % of each run)

| Arch / size | params | sps | wall for 6 k steps |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| transformer-small @ max_len=256 | 4.2 M | **85** | 73 s |
| transformer-small @ max_len=768 (Phase-2+) | 4.2 M | 31 | 200 s |
| transformer-medium @ max_len=256 | 33.6 M | 24 | 250 s |
| transformer-medium @ max_len=768 (Phase-2+) | 33.6 M | 11 | 545 s |
| transformer-large @ max_len=256 | 113.4 M | 10 | 600 s |
| xLSTM-small @ max_len=256 | 1.7 M | 31 | 195 s |
| xLSTM-medium @ max_len=256 | 12.1 M | 11 | 550 s |

**Findings:**

- `max_len = 256 → 768` ≈ 3× slower per step (attention is O(L²)). Worth it for the +19 pp Top-1 lift.
- xLSTM at the same param count is **3-4× slower than transformer** with no quality benefit. The sLSTM block's sequential CUDA kernel dominates wall.
- Step-as-token achieves higher sps but compromises OOD on the hidden 4th family — chose compositional everywhere despite the cost.

## Where the time actually went

| Activity | % of total |
|---|--:|
| Training cells (104 of them) | ~23 % |
| Eval generation (per-cell, %-examples=100, beam-search) | ~62 % |
| Submission CSV generation | ~4 % |
| Headroom / waiting in queue | ~11 % |

**Bottleneck**: compositional beam search inference (~2-3 s per example). Cutting `--max-examples` from 100 → 50 would have halved eval wall with minimal loss of statistical power.

---

## Key efficiency lessons we'd carry to the next hackathon

1. **Always validate sequence-length distribution against `max_len` before launching a grid.** The max_len=256 bug ate ~7 A100-hours of Phase-1 training that we had to redo as Phase-2.
2. **Eval-time `--max-examples=100` is overkill on highly-structured data.** n=50 gives ±4 pp confidence intervals — enough for ranking recipes.
3. **Drop architectures that show identical convergence loss to the smaller faster option.** xLSTM cost us ~3 A100-hours that we could've spent on physics injection.
4. **`.pixi/` env on `$SCRATCH` survives bootstraps if you exclude it from `rsync --delete`.** Initial bug cost us 10 min × 4 bootstraps = ~40 min.
5. **Use `--array=N%4` array job throttling religiously.** We never queued behind ourselves; SLURM spread cells across multiple nodes when capacity permitted.
